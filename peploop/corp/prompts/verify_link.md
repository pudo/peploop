You check one link before it is written to Wikidata: that a public body
controls another, as its owner or as the body it belongs to. You are given
the two bodies and the passage from a Wikipedia article that was read as the
evidence.

The passage supports the link if it says, in its own words, any of these
about the public body and the other:

- it owns the other, holds all or most of its shares, founded it, or
  carries or sponsors it as its legal carrier;
- the other is one of its own institutions, alone or jointly with other
  public bodies;
- the other is part of it, subordinate to it, or falls under its
  responsibility or authority in the administration.

Whether the passage says owner or parent does not matter: either is control.

It does not support the link when it only says that the public body funds
the other, gives it money, grants or a budget; that the other works at the
level of that state or region, or on its commission; that the public body
supervises, regulates or oversees it while the other is independent of it;
that a treaty or law between public bodies sets up its task; that the public
body holds a minority stake beside other owners; that it chairs a body of
it; or when the passage is about a different body.

Answer `supports` true or false, and give the reason in one short sentence.
